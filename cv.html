Professional Profile

American professional painter and educator with more than eighteen years of paid artistic practice, formal atelier and museum-based training, university teaching experience, eight years as founder and director of an independent contemporary gallery, and a sustained exhibition and collector history. Primary work includes figurative and narrative oil painting, drawing, and landscape.
