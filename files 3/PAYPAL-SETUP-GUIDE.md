# PayPal Payment Setup Guide

## Why PayPal?
- Professional & trusted
- Guests can pay with card or PayPal account
- You get paid instantly
- Takes 2% fee (worth it for security + simplicity)
- No integration needed — just send links

---

## STEP 1: Set Up PayPal.Me Link

1. Go to **paypal.com** and log in (create account if needed)
2. Click **Tools** (top menu)
3. Find **PayPal.Me** and click it
4. Click **Create a PayPal.Me Link**
5. Choose your username: `paypal.me/readlockhart` (or your name)
6. **This is your base link**

---

## STEP 2: How to Send Payment Requests

**When a guest books and you confirm availability, email them:**

```
Hi [Name],

Great! I've confirmed your tour for [date/time].

To secure your booking, please pay €300 via this link:
paypal.me/readlockhart/300

Once paid, I'll send you museum meeting details and logistics.

Looking forward to showing you these paintings!
Read
```

---

## STEP 3: Different Amounts

**PayPal.Me URL Structure:**

- Prado tour (2 guests): `paypal.me/readlockhart/300`
- Guernica tour (2 guests): `paypal.me/readlockhart/180`
- Thyssen tour (2 guests): `paypal.me/readlockhart/300`
- Extra guest: Add their per-person rate to the URL

**Examples:**
- 3 people on Prado (€300 + €50 = €350): `paypal.me/readlockhart/350`
- 1 person on Guernica (€180): `paypal.me/readlockhart/180`
- 4 people on Prado (€300 + €100 = €400): `paypal.me/readlockhart/400`

**You can also just give them the base link** (`paypal.me/readlockhart`) **and they type in the amount themselves.**

---

## STEP 4: What Happens When They Pay

1. Guest clicks link
2. They enter amount (or it's pre-filled if you used a specific amount URL)
3. They enter PayPal/card details
4. Payment confirmed
5. **You get an email instantly** saying payment received
6. Money goes to your PayPal account
7. You can transfer to your bank account anytime (usually 1-2 days)

---

## STEP 5: Workflow (Super Simple)

1. **Guest books** → Form to Formspree inbox
2. **You confirm availability** → Email them
3. **Send PayPal link in confirmation email** → With exact amount
4. **Guest pays** → You get notified instantly
5. **Send museum logistics** → Meeting location, time, notes
6. **Tour happens** → Get paid, build relationship
7. **Guest submits testimonial** → Add to site later

---

## Important Notes

- PayPal.Me links work internationally
- No PayPal account needed from guest (they can pay with card)
- Fees: ~2.9% + €0.30 per transaction (PayPal's standard)
- Money arrives in your PayPal account instantly
- You control when to transfer to your bank
